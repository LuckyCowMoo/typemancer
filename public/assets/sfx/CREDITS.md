# Typemancer Sound Effects & Credits

All sound effects used in Typemancer are sourced from open-source game asset platforms under permissive, public domain and Creative Commons licenses:

## 1. 80 CC0 RPG SFX
- **Creator:** rubberduck (OpenGameArt.org)
- **License:** Creative Commons CC0 1.0 Universal (Public Domain)
- **URL:** https://opengameart.org/content/80-cc0-rpg-sfx
- **Used for:** Slashing blades (`blade_01.ogg`, `blade_02.ogg`, `blade_03.ogg`), elemental fire spells (`spell_fire_01.ogg`..`03`), boulder crashes (`stones_01.ogg`), binding chains (`chain_01.ogg`, `chain_02.ogg`), monster roars (`creature_roar_01.ogg`, `creature_roar_02.ogg`, `creature_monster_01.ogg`), slime/acid spells (`creature_slime_01.ogg`, `creature_slime_02.ogg`), and manuscripts (`book_01.ogg`..`03`).

## 2. Kenney RPG Audio
- **Creator:** Kenney (Kenney.nl)
- **License:** Creative Commons CC0 1.0 Universal (Public Domain)
- **URL:** https://kenney.nl/assets/rpg-audio
- **Used for:** Cleaves (`chop.ogg`), precision slices (`knifeSlice.ogg`, `knifeSlice2.ogg`), quick draws (`drawKnife1.ogg`), book flips and slams (`bookClose.ogg`, `bookPlace1.ogg`, `bookPlace2.ogg`, `bookFlip1.ogg`..`03`), typewriter and metallic actions (`metalPot1.ogg`..`03`, `metalClick.ogg`, `metalLatch.ogg`), and evasive cloth movements (`clothBelt.ogg`, `clothBelt2.ogg`, `cloth1.ogg`, `cloth4.ogg`).
